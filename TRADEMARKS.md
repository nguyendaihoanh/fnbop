### Allowed without asking
- Saying that your product or service is "based on FnBOps", "compatible with FnBOps", or "a fork of FnBOps".
- Using the name and logo **unmodified** in articles, tutorials, talks, and links to the official repository.
- Running the official, unmodified FnBOps in your own store, with the original branding left as-is.

### Requires written permission (hoanh@vnaigc.com)

### Forks
Forks are welcome under AGPL-3.0-only. A public fork that is distributed or hosted for other people should **use its own name and logo**, while keeping the attribution required by [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) (Section 7(b)).
